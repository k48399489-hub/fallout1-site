Game Overview

Fallout (1997) — A post-apocalyptic role-playing game by Interplay. Explore the wasteland, make tough choices, and manage your S.P.E.C.I.A.L. stats as you search for a water chip for Vault 13.

Release1997
DeveloperInterplay Productions
GenreCRPG / Post-apocalyptic
PlatformPC (Windows 95/98)

Characters & Notable NPCs

From the steadfast Vault Dweller to the scheming Aradesh and the brutal Deathclaws, Fallout's cast is memorable. Click a name to reveal a short bio.

The player character — the Vault Dweller leaves Vault 13 to find a replacement water chip and encounters moral choices that shape the Wasteland.
Aradesh — leader of Shady Sands, involved in political intrigue and alliance-building.
Deathclaws — terrifying mutated predators; best avoided until well-equipped.

Vaults & Locations

Vault 13 — the starting Vault. Shady Sands, Junktown, and the Den are key settlements with their own quests and characters.

  • Vault 13 — water chip quest
  • Shady Sands — early friendly town
  • Junktown — law and chaos mixed
  • Brotherhood of Steel Outpost — advanced tech
